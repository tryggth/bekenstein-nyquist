\begin{abstract}
The Bekenstein--Nyquist Shock Worldline Conjecture (BNSWC) establishes a rigorous mathematical bridge between relativistic information density bounds, microlocal partial differential equation (PDE) theory, and Boolean or unitary circuit complexity. The conjecture is founded on three physical premises---the Bekenstein spatial information bound ($\Lambda_{\text{Bek}}$), the Margolus--Levitin rate limit on local state changes ($\nu_{\text{ML}}$), and reflectionless local unitarity ($U^\dagger U = I$)---formalized as four minimal axioms (Axioms A1--A4) under scale-generating nonlinearity ($[P_{\Lambda}, U_{\text{nl}}] \ne 0$). Smooth, sub-Nyquist field configurations evolve via spatially parallel, constant-depth updates ($\mathcal{O}(1)$ circuit depth), whereas nonlinear convective steepening at a hydrodynamic or field-theoretic shock front saturates the local spatial bandwidth. Rather than postulating auxiliary degrees of freedom by hand, we prove via Stinespring/Sz.-Nagy dilation (\texttt{Unitary\allowbreak{}Bandwidth\allowbreak{}Depth.lean}) that preserving local unitarity without violating spatial bandwidth bounds mathematically forces the existence of an orthogonal worldline clock fiber $\mathcal{H}_{\text{clock}} = V_{\Lambda}^{\perp}$ of rank at least $r = \mathrm{rank}([P_{\Lambda}, U_{\text{nl}}])/2$, a $90^\circ$ microlocal signature rotation in the complexified cotangent bundle across the saturation horizon $\Sigma(x,t) = 0$, and an effective Landau--Ginzburg--Higgs potential $V_{\text{eff}}(c) = \Sigma_0 |c|^2 + \lambda |c|^4$. This monograph presents the unified axiomatic architecture (including the equivalence between the Emergent Stinespring Dilation formulation and the Primitive Bipartite Fiber-Bundle formulation), the five-phase analytical proofs, the machine-checked Lean 4 verification, the numerical verification across four progressive hydrodynamic lattice benchmarks, the holographic Ryu--Takayanagi bulk-extrusion correspondence, and the formal duality with the Landau--Ginzburg--Higgs mechanism.
\end{abstract}

\subsection{Analytical Proof Architecture for the Nyquist--Lax Trichotomy}
\label{subsec:nyquist-lax-trichotomy}
To prove that spatial parallelism breaks down at a shock front under Axioms A1, A3, and A4, notice that ``local unitarity'' purely within the spatial Hilbert space $\mathcal{H}_{\Lambda}$ combines two distinct operational requirements: norm conservation ($\|\Phi_{\Delta t}(u)\|_2 = \|u\|_2$) and bounded-fan-in spatial locality ($\mathcal{O}(1)$ parallel circuit depth within stencil radius $r_0$). \begin{theorem}[Nyquist--Lax Trichotomy / Aliasing--Unitarity No-Go Lemma]
\label{thm:nyquist-lax-trichotomy}
Any single-step spatial update operator $\Phi_{\Delta t} : \mathcal{H}_{\Lambda} \to \mathcal{H}_{\Lambda}$ acting across a shock locus where $\mathcal{A}_{\Lambda}[u] = \|[P_{\Lambda}, [M(u), L(u)]]\|_{\text{HS}} > \Theta_{\text{DSW}}$ must violate at least one of the following three operational postulates:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Postulate 1 --- Strict Spatial Bandwidth (Bekenstein Bound, Axiom A1):} $\mathrm{supp}(\hat{u}) \subseteq [-\Lambda_{\text{Bek}}, \Lambda_{\text{Bek}}]$ without dynamically allocating sub-Planckian spatial lattice nodes.
\item \textbf{Postulate 2 --- Norm Unitarity / Information Conservation (First Half of Axiom A4):} $\|\Phi_{\Delta t}(u)\|_2 = \|u\|_2$, forbidding artificial viscosity, WENO truncation, or Landauer erasure of ultraviolet modes.
\item \textbf{Postulate 3 --- Constant Parallel Depth / Bounded-Fan-In Spatial Locality (Second Half of Local Unitarity):} $\Phi_{\Delta t}$ is computable in circuit depth $\mathcal{O}(1)$ purely within $\mathcal{H}_{\Lambda}$ with a spatial stencil fan-in bounded by a fixed sub-Nyquist radius $r_0$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}[Proof Architecture]
Assume Postulates 1, 2, and 3 hold simultaneously on $\mathcal{H}_{\Lambda}$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Step 1 (Balian--Low and Donoho--Stark Uncertainty Barrier):} By the quantitative Balian--Low theorem and the Donoho--Stark uncertainty inequality ($|T|\cdot |\Omega| \ge 1 - (\epsilon_T + \epsilon_{\Omega})^2$), a shock discontinuity compressed to spatial width $\delta x \sim \pi / \Lambda_{\text{Bek}}$ concentrates $\mathcal{O}(1)$ spectral weight at the Brillouin boundary $|k| \in [\Lambda_{\text{Bek}}/2, \Lambda_{\text{Bek}}]$, generating a non-zero inter-band leakage operator $A = (I - P_{\Lambda}) U_{\text{nl}} P_{\Lambda} : \mathcal{H}_{\Lambda} \to \mathcal{H}_{2\Lambda} \ominus \mathcal{H}_{\Lambda}$.

Thus, any bandlimited update $\Phi_{\Delta t} = P_{\Lambda} U_{\text{nl}} P_{\Lambda}$ dissipates norm $\|\Phi_{\Delta t} u\|_2^2 = \|u\|_2^2 - \|A u\|_2^2 < \|u\|_2^2$ (violating Postulate 2) whenever $A u \ne 0$.
\item \textbf{Step 3 (GNVW Chiral Index Obstruction and Group Velocity Reversal):} By the Gross--Nesme--Vogts--Werner (GNVW) index theorem for 1D Quantum Cellular Automata, any strictly local constant-depth unitary circuit restricted purely to $\mathcal{H}_{\Lambda}$ (Postulate 3) has a trivial inter-subspace flow index between $\mathcal{H}_{\Lambda}$ and $\mathcal{H}_{2\Lambda} \ominus \mathcal{H}_{\Lambda}$. Folding the chiral ultraviolet flux back into $[-\Lambda_{\text{Bek}}, \Lambda_{\text{Bek}}]$ via Umklapp aliasing ($k \mapsto k - 2\Lambda_{\text{Bek}}$) reverses the group velocity $v_g = \partial\omega/\partial k < 0$ at $|k| = \Lambda_{\text{Bek}}$ and produces non-physical backward dispersive ringing. Preserving both Postulates 1 and 2 therefore requires Stinespring dilation into an orthogonal register $\mathcal{H}_{\text{clock}}$ across sequential depth $D \ge \mathrm{rank}(A)$ (violating Postulate 3) ticked at the Margolus--Levitin rate limit $\nu_{\text{ML}}$ (Axiom A2).
\end{enumerate}
\end{proof}
