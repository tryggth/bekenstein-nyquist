\begin{abstract}
The Bekenstein--Nyquist Shock Worldline Conjecture (BNSWC) establishes a rigorous mathematical bridge between bandlimited effective field theory (EFT), microlocal partial differential equation (PDE) analysis, and Boolean or unitary circuit complexity. Motivated by three foundational principles---the Bekenstein--Kempf spatial bandwidth cutoff ($\Lambda_{\text{Bek}}$)~\cite{bekenstein1981,casini2008,kempf2004}, the Margolus--Levitin rate limit on local state transitions ($\nu_{\text{ML}}$)~\cite{margolus1998,mandelstam1945}, and reflectionless local unitarity ($U^\dagger U = I$)~\cite{landauer1961,bennett1973}---we formalize a bandlimited lattice EFT via four minimal axioms (Axioms A1--A4) under scale-generating nonlinearity ($[P_{\Lambda}, U_{\text{nl}}] \ne 0$)~\cite{lax1968}. Smooth, sub-Nyquist field configurations ($\Sigma(x,t) > 0$) propagate causally in $\mathcal{O}(1)$ parallel circuit depth per macro-step, whereas nonlinear convective steepening at a hydrodynamic shock front ($\Sigma(x,t) < 0$) saturates the local spatial bandwidth. Rather than postulating auxiliary degrees of freedom ad hoc, we prove via Stinespring and Sz.-Nagy dilation~\cite{stinespring1955,sznagy1953} (\texttt{Unitary\allowbreak{}Bandwidth\allowbreak{}Depth.lean}) that preserving local unitarity without violating spatial bandlimitation mathematically forces the existence of an orthogonal worldline clock fiber $\mathcal{H}_{\text{clock}} = V_{\Lambda}^{\perp}$ of rank at least $r = \mathrm{rank}([P_{\Lambda}, U_{\text{nl}}])/2$, a $90^\circ$ microlocal continuation in the complexified cotangent bundle across the sonic/Nyquist horizon $\Sigma(x,t) = 0$, and a localized Ginzburg--Landau shock-core defect-condensate potential $V_{\text{eff}}(|c|^2) = \Sigma_0 |c|^2 + \lambda |c|^4$~\cite{ginzburg1950} that is algebraically isomorphic to the Standard Model Higgs potential~\cite{higgs1964,tooby2024} under $\mu^2 = -\Sigma_0$. This monograph presents the unified axiomatic architecture, the five-phase analytical proofs, six machine-checked Lean 4 modules (\texttt{Mathlib}, \texttt{Physlib}, and \texttt{QuantumInfo})~\cite{mathlib2020,tooby2024}, four progressive hydrodynamic lattice benchmarks, the holographic Ryu--Takayanagi bulk-extrusion correspondence~\cite{ryutakayanagi2006,swingle2012}, and the Ginzburg--Landau--Higgs defect-condensate duality.
\end{abstract}

The Bekenstein--Nyquist Shock Worldline Conjecture investigates a bandlimited lattice effective field theory (EFT) motivated by simultaneous enforcement of three foundational principles of fundamental physics that have historically been analyzed in isolation:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{The Bekenstein--Kempf Spatial Bandwidth Cutoff ($\Lambda_{\text{Bek}}$):} Black hole thermodynamics and algebraic quantum field theory establish the Bekenstein bound~\cite{bekenstein1981,casini2008}, restricting the maximum information $I_{\max}$ storable within a region of radius $R$ and energy $E$ to
\begin{equation}
I_{\max} \le \frac{2\pi E R}{\hbar c \ln 2}.
\end{equation}
Following Achim Kempf's covariant bandlimitation framework~\cite{kempf2000,kempf2004,pye2015}, we treat a finite local spatial information density as motivating an effective ultraviolet wavenumber cutoff $|k| \le \Lambda_{\text{Bek}}$, restricting observable continuum fields to a Paley--Wiener Hilbert space $\mathcal{H}_{\Lambda} = PW_{\Lambda_{\text{Bek}}}(\mathbb{R})$ obeying Bernstein's inequality $\|u^{(r)}\|_2 \le \Lambda_{\text{Bek}}^r \|u\|_2$. By the Shannon--Nyquist sampling theorem~\cite{shannon1949}, $\mathcal{H}_{\Lambda}$ is unitarily isomorphic to a discrete sampling lattice $\ell^2(\mathbb{Z}_{\Delta x})$ of fundamental spacing $\Delta x = \pi / \Lambda_{\text{Bek}}$.
\item \textbf{The Margolus--Levitin Rate Limit on State Changes ($\nu_{\text{ML}}$):} Quantum mechanics enforces the Mandelstam--Tamm~\cite{mandelstam1945} and Margolus--Levitin~\cite{margolus1998,lloyd2000} quantum speed limits, which bound the maximum frequency of distinguishable transitions between orthogonal quantum states by
\begin{equation}
\nu_{\text{ML}} \le \frac{2E}{\pi \hbar}, \qquad \Delta\tau_{\text{ML}} = \frac{\pi}{\nu_{\text{ML}}} \ge \frac{\pi^2 \hbar}{2E}.
\end{equation}
Within our bandlimited lattice EFT, the spatial cutoff $\Lambda_{\text{Bek}}$ and the local gate-rate ceiling $\nu_{\text{ML}}$ act as fixed ultraviolet/rate parameters defining a local spacetime information-processing capacity per unit length per unit time:
\begin{equation}
C_{\text{self}} \equiv \theta_{\text{Nyq}} = \Lambda_{\text{Bek}} \nu_{\text{ML}}.
\end{equation}
\item \textbf{Local Microscopic Unitarity vs.\ Landauer Erasure ($U^\dagger U = I$):} Fundamental quantum evolution is strictly unitary and causally local (bounded spatial fan-in radius $r_0$ per parallel step~\cite{liebrobinson1972}), conserving total Hilbert-space norm and phase-space volume~\cite{bennett1973}. While linear dispersive waves propagate trivially inside $\mathcal{H}_{\Lambda}$, nonlinear convective steepening (such as $u\partial_x u$ in Burgers/Euler hydrodynamics~\cite{lax1973,whitham1974}) multiplies modes in the upper half of the Brillouin zone ($\Lambda_{\text{Bek}}/2 < |k| \le \Lambda_{\text{Bek}}$), driving local ultraviolet spillover flux $\mathcal{F}_{tx}(x,t) = \|P_{\text{UV}}(u\partial_x u)(x,t)\|$ toward the forbidden super-Nyquist zone $(\Lambda_{\text{Bek}}, 2\Lambda_{\text{Bek}}]$. Standard continuum hydrodynamics stabilizes shocks via viscous dissipation~\cite{lax1973} (violating unitarity via Landauer bit erasure $E_{\min} = n k_B T \ln 2$~\cite{landauer1961}), whereas naive unitary lattice truncation reflects ultraviolet modes back into the bulk via Umklapp aliasing (producing dispersive Gibbs/Lax--Levermore oscillations~\cite{laxlevermore1983}).
\end{enumerate}
The central thesis of this monograph is that a bandlimited unitary system resolves nonlinear shock steepening under these three constraints without Landauer erasure or Gibbs aliasing by dynamically trading \textbf{spatial parallelism} for \textbf{sequential temporal circuit depth} along the lower-dimensional shock worldline.

\subsection{The Minimal Four-Axiom Formulation (Emergent Dilation Architecture)}
\label{subsec:minimal-axioms}
To separate physical resource inputs from derived mathematical structures, we formulate the conjecture on a composite Hilbert space $\mathcal{H}_{\text{tot}}$ governed by four primitive axioms: two kinematic resource cutoffs (Bekenstein--Kempf and Margolus--Levitin), one dynamical nonlinearity condition, and one local unitarity conservation law.
\begin{axiom}[Spatial Bandlimitation --- The Bekenstein--Kempf Projector (A1)]
\label{ax:a1}
Observable spatial fields reside in a Paley--Wiener subspace $V_{\Lambda} = P_{\Lambda}\mathcal{H}_{\text{tot}} = PW_{\Lambda_{\text{Bek}}}(\mathbb{R})$~\cite{kempf2004,pye2015} defined by an idempotent, self-adjoint ultraviolet wavenumber projector $P_{\Lambda}^2 = P_{\Lambda} = P_{\Lambda}^\dagger$ with hard spectral cutoff $|k| \le \Lambda_{\text{Bek}}$, unitarily isomorphic to the discrete sampling lattice $\ell^2(\mathbb{Z}_{\Delta x})$ with fundamental spacing $\Delta x = \pi / \Lambda_{\text{Bek}}$~\cite{shannon1949}.
\end{axiom}
\begin{axiom}[Rate Limit on State Changes --- The Margolus--Levitin Sequential Channel (A2)]
\label{ax:a2}
Any local state transition coupling the bandlimited spatial subspace $V_{\Lambda}$ to an orthogonal degree of freedom proceeds through elementary rank-1 quantum channels ($\mathrm{rank}((I - P_{\Lambda}) W_k P_{\Lambda}) \le 1$) whose transition frequency is bounded above by the Margolus--Levitin rate parameter $\nu_{\text{ML}} \le \frac{2E}{\pi\hbar}$~\cite{margolus1998,mandelstam1945} (imposing a minimum sequential clock tick $\Delta\tau_{\text{ML}} = \pi / \nu_{\text{ML}}$ and maximum single-step rotation angle $\vartheta_{\max}$ per elementary gate $W_k$).
\end{axiom}
\begin{axiom}[Scale-Generating Nonlinearity --- Non-Commutativity (A3)]
\label{ax:a3}
The microscopic generator of spatial wave transport is nonlinear (convective mode-multiplying), so for steepening wave profiles the unconstrained short-time propagator $U_{\text{nl}} = \exp(\Delta t M(u))$ fails to commute with the spatial bandlimit projector $P_{\Lambda}$~\cite{lax1968}:
\begin{equation}
[P_{\Lambda}, U_{\text{nl}}] \ne 0 \quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad \mathcal{A}_{\Lambda}[u] \equiv \left\| \big[ P_{\Lambda}, [M(u), L(u)] \big] \right\|_{\text{HS}} > 0,
\end{equation}
where $(L(u), M(u))$ denotes the Lax pair~\cite{lax1968} of the nonlinear flow and $F_{tx}[u] = \partial_t M - \partial_x L + [M, L]$ is the zero-curvature defect, with pointwise ultraviolet spillover envelope density $\mathcal{F}_{tx}(x,t) = \|P_{\text{UV}}(u\partial_x u)(x,t)\|$.
\end{axiom}
\begin{axiom}[Reflectionless Local Unitarity --- No Erasure and No Aliasing (A4)]
\label{ax:a4}
Time evolution on $\mathcal{H}_{\text{tot}}$ is generated by bounded-fan-in local unitary operators ($U^\dagger U = I$, forbidding dissipative Landauer bit erasure $E_{\min} = n k_B T \ln 2$~\cite{landauer1961,bennett1973}) that satisfy reflectionless impedance matching at the Brillouin boundary $|k| = \Lambda_{\text{Bek}}$ (forbidding unphysical Umklapp spectral folding $k \mapsto k - 2\Lambda_{\text{Bek}}$ or backward dispersive Gibbs reflections into $V_{\Lambda}$).
\end{axiom}

\subsection{Analytical Proof Architecture for the Nyquist--Lax Trichotomy}
\label{subsec:nyquist-lax-trichotomy}
To prove that spatial parallelism breaks down at a shock front under Axioms A1, A3, and A4, notice that ``local unitarity'' purely within the spatial Hilbert space $\mathcal{H}_{\Lambda}$ combines two distinct operational requirements: norm conservation ($\|\Phi_{\Delta t}(u)\|_2 = \|u\|_2$) and bounded-fan-in spatial locality ($\mathcal{O}(1)$ parallel circuit depth within stencil radius $r_0$~\cite{liebrobinson1972}). Theorem \ref{thm:nyquist-lax-trichotomy} proves that at a super-critical shock front, these two halves of spatial local unitarity cannot coexist with the Bekenstein--Kempf bandlimit (Axiom A1), forcing the relaxation of $\mathcal{O}(1)$ parallel depth into sequential time-stepping governed by the Margolus--Levitin rate limit (Axiom A2).
\begin{theorem}[Nyquist--Lax Trichotomy / Aliasing--Unitarity No-Go Lemma]
\label{thm:nyquist-lax-trichotomy}
Any single-step spatial update operator $\Phi_{\Delta t} : \mathcal{H}_{\Lambda} \to \mathcal{H}_{\Lambda}$ acting across a shock locus where $\mathcal{A}_{\Lambda}[u] = \|[P_{\Lambda}, [M(u), L(u)]]\|_{\text{HS}} > \Theta_{\text{DSW}}$ must violate at least one of the following three operational postulates:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Postulate 1 --- Strict Spatial Bandwidth (Bekenstein--Kempf Bound, Axiom A1):} $\mathrm{supp}(\hat{u}) \subseteq [-\Lambda_{\text{Bek}}, \Lambda_{\text{Bek}}]$ without dynamically allocating sub-Planckian spatial lattice nodes.
\item \textbf{Postulate 2 --- Norm Unitarity / Information Conservation (First Half of Axiom A4):} $\|\Phi_{\Delta t}(u)\|_2 = \|u\|_2$, forbidding artificial viscosity, WENO truncation, or Landauer erasure~\cite{landauer1961} of ultraviolet modes.
\item \textbf{Postulate 3 --- Constant Parallel Depth / Bounded-Fan-In Spatial Locality (Second Half of Local Unitarity):} $\Phi_{\Delta t}$ is computable in circuit depth $\mathcal{O}(1)$ purely within $\mathcal{H}_{\Lambda}$ with a spatial stencil fan-in bounded by a fixed sub-Nyquist radius $r_0$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}[Proof Architecture]
Assume Postulates 1, 2, and 3 hold simultaneously on $\mathcal{H}_{\Lambda}$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Step 1 (Donoho--Stark Uncertainty Barrier):} By the Donoho--Stark uncertainty inequality~\cite{donohostark1989} ($|T|\cdot |\Omega| \ge 1 - (\epsilon_T + \epsilon_{\Omega})^2$), a shock discontinuity compressed to spatial width $\delta x \sim \pi / \Lambda_{\text{Bek}}$ concentrates $\mathcal{O}(1)$ spectral weight at the Brillouin boundary $|k| \in [\Lambda_{\text{Bek}}/2, \Lambda_{\text{Bek}}]$, generating a non-zero inter-band leakage operator $A = (I - P_{\Lambda}) U_{\text{nl}} P_{\Lambda} : \mathcal{H}_{\Lambda} \to \mathcal{H}_{2\Lambda} \ominus \mathcal{H}_{\Lambda}$.
\item \textbf{Step 2 (Unitary Defect Identity):} Decomposing the unconstrained unitary propagator $U_{\text{nl}} = \exp(\Delta t M(u))$ on $\mathcal{H}_{\Lambda} \oplus \mathcal{H}_{\Lambda}^{\perp}$ yields $(P_{\Lambda} U_{\text{nl}} P_{\Lambda})^\dagger (P_{\Lambda} U_{\text{nl}} P_{\Lambda}) = P_{\Lambda} - A^\dagger A$. Thus, any bandlimited update $\Phi_{\Delta t} = P_{\Lambda} U_{\text{nl}} P_{\Lambda}$ dissipates norm $\|\Phi_{\Delta t} u\|_2^2 = \|u\|_2^2 - \|A u\|_2^2 < \|u\|_2^2$ (violating Postulate 2) whenever $A u \ne 0$.
\item \textbf{Step 3 (GNVW Chiral Index Obstruction and Group Velocity Reversal):} By the Gross--Nesme--Vogts--Werner (GNVW) index theorem for 1D Quantum Cellular Automata~\cite{gnvw2012}, any strictly local constant-depth unitary circuit restricted purely to $\mathcal{H}_{\Lambda}$ (Postulate 3) has a trivial inter-subspace flow index between $\mathcal{H}_{\Lambda}$ and $\mathcal{H}_{2\Lambda} \ominus \mathcal{H}_{\Lambda}$. Folding the chiral ultraviolet flux back into $[-\Lambda_{\text{Bek}}, \Lambda_{\text{Bek}}]$ via Umklapp aliasing ($k \mapsto k - 2\Lambda_{\text{Bek}}$) reverses the group velocity $v_g = \partial\omega/\partial k < 0$ at $|k| = \Lambda_{\text{Bek}}$ and produces non-physical backward dispersive ringing~\cite{laxlevermore1983}. Preserving both Postulates 1 and 2 therefore requires Stinespring dilation~\cite{stinespring1955} into an orthogonal register $\mathcal{H}_{\text{clock}}$ across sequential depth $D \ge \mathrm{rank}(A)$ (violating Postulate 3) ticked at the Margolus--Levitin rate limit $\nu_{\text{ML}}$~\cite{margolus1998} (Axiom A2).
\end{enumerate}
